About Tender-IQ

Tender‑IQ is a beginner‑friendly NLP project that demonstrates how to extract structured information from government or corporate tender documents.

Why this project?

Small businesses and contractors often spend hours reading lengthy tender documents. Manual review is error‑prone and time‑consuming. By applying simple NLP techniques – tokenisation, regular‑expression patterns and rule‑based classification – we can highlight the most important sections and help users focus on the critical requirements.

Features

  • Upload PDF or plain‑text tender documents.
  • Automatic extraction of title, reference, dates, financial figures, eligibility criteria and mandatory documents.
  • Risk alerts for conflicting dates or missing mandatory items.
  • Clean, responsive dashboard built with Bootstrap 5.
  • Downloadable JSON/TXT summary for offline review.
